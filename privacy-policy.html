RTO Ready: Licence Exam India Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

RTO Ready: Licence Exam India does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

About this policy

This privacy policy explains how RTO Ready: Licence Exam India, published by General GoldenVision, handles information when you use the Android application.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or sensitive user data. The app does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected State or Union Territory, the number of tests and answers completed, your best score, and the identifiers of questions answered incorrectly. This information remains on your device and is used only to provide progress and review features.

You can delete this locally stored information at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Data sharing

We do not transmit or share user data with General GoldenVision or any third party.

Children's privacy

RTO Ready: Licence Exam India does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other user.

Government affiliation and information source

RTO Ready: Licence Exam India is an independent study app. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, any government authority. Users should verify current rules and test requirements with their State RTO.

The question bank is based on the public sample question bank published by Parivahan Sewa, Ministry of Road Transport & Highways. The source can be reviewed at parivahan.gov.in.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will always appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or RTO Ready: Licence Exam India, contact General GoldenVision at goldenvisofficial@gmail.com.
